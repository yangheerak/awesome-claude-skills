# The 15-Minute Coffee Chat

Free, short, and genuinely useful. It turns a follower into a relationship. It isn't a sales call.

## Booking Setup

- Tool: Calendly / Cal.com / TidyCal (free tier). Event name: **"Free 15-min Coffee Chat — Moving to Korea"**
- Two event types:
  - **In person (in Korea):** 15 min, 2–3 fixed cafés you like (part of your personal touch), limited weekly slots
  - **Video (not in Korea yet):** 15 min, Google Meet / Zoom
- Buffer 15 min between chats; max 5–8 per week so quality stays high
- Intake questions (required):
  1. Where are you now, and when are you moving / did you arrive?
  2. Visa type (or "not sure")
  3. City / area you need to live near (school, office)
  4. Monthly budget and available deposit (보증금)
  5. What's your #1 problem right now?
  6. How did you find me? (for attribution)
- Consent checkbox: "I agree to be contacted about my move and, if I ask for it, introduced to relevant housing providers or services." (see privacy in `compliance-korea.md`)
- Confirmation message: "See you at [café]. I'll grab a table near the window. Bring any listings or contracts you're unsure about."

## Pre-Chat Prep (Claude generates this from intake)

```
Name / visa / arrival date / area / budget / deposit
Likely housing options for this budget + area (3)
Likely admin problems for this visa (ARC, bank, phone)
Relevant partners from partner-directory.csv (not to pitch, just ready)
One free resource to send after
```

## Agenda (15 minutes)

| Min | Step | What you say / do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Warm up | Personal, not scripted. Where they're from, why Korea. |
| 2–7 | Diagnose | "What's stressing you most about the move?" → then "What have you tried?" → "What does a good outcome look like in 30 days?" |
| 7–12 | Help for real | Answer their #1 problem on the spot. Give a clear next step they can do alone. |
| 12–14 | Plan | "Here's what I'll send you after this: [free win #1]." Ask permission to follow up. |
| 14–15 | Close | "If you ever want me to handle the housing part end-to-end, I do that as a paid service, but no pressure. Let's get you the checklist first." |

Rules: listen more than you talk. Don't pitch partners in the first chat unless they ask. Write notes right after.

## After the Chat (within 24h)

Message template:
> Hi [Name], great meeting you at [café]! As promised, here's [checklist / shortlist / explainer]. Based on what you said about [their words], I'd start with [step 1]. Reply here if anything is confusing. Happy to help with the next step too.

Log in tracker: date, problem, promised next step, free win #1 delivered (Y/N), temperature (hot / warm / cold).

## Free Win Ladder

1. Personalized resource (checklist, area comparison, deposit explainer)
2. Personalized shortlist (rooms, areas, co-living options) or document help
3. Warm intro to a vetted partner with context (so the partner treats them well)

After win #3 → offer the paid package (see `pricing-and-offers.md`).

## Handling Common Situations

- **"Can you just find me a room?"** → "Yes, that's my Move-In Package. Here's what's included and the price."
- **"Is this landlord legit?"** → give the general checks (등기부등본, deposit insurance availability, contract in their name); for a formal contract review, route to the licensed agent partner.
- **Legal / visa questions** → give general info, then refer to 1345 (Immigration Contact Center), Seoul Global Center, or a licensed 행정사. Don't give legal advice.
- **No-show** → one friendly rebook message, then let it go.
