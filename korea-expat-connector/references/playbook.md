# Playbook: From Zero to 10 Paying Customers

A 90-day plan, then a scale plan. Each phase has a goal, the actions, and a hard exit criterion. Don't skip ahead: a phase you skip usually shows up again later as the reason something failed.

---

## §0. Positioning (Days 1–3)

**Goal:** Know exactly who you help and why you.

1. Write the one-sentence promise:
   > "I help [segment] move to [city/area] in Korea without [top pain], by giving you free guides and connecting you with people I've personally checked."
2. Pick 3 audience segments to start (you can widen later):

   | Segment | Visa (typical) | Top pains | What they'll pay for |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | International students | D-2, D-4 | Deposit (보증금) too high, dorm vs goshiwon vs share house, ARC, phone | Room search + viewing support |
   | English teachers / new hires | E-2, E-7 | Housing near work, key money, contract in Korean, banking | Contract review via agent partner, move-in setup |
   | Remote workers / digital nomads | Workation visa (F-1-D), short stays | 1–3 month stays, co-working, community | Co-living placement, monthly stay bundles |
   | Spouses / families | F-6, F-3 | Schools, family-sized apartments, hospitals | Area tour + agent intro |

3. Pick your angle (your "personal taste"): your neighborhood, your story, your language, your style. Examples: "the Korean friend who tells you the stuff agents won't," "the foreigner who got scammed on a 전세 so you don't," "Seoul on a student budget."
4. Set up the minimum stack:
   - One handle across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube (Shorts + long-form)
   - Link-in-bio page with: free checklist download, 15-min coffee chat booking, WhatsApp/KakaoTalk contact
   - Booking tool (Calendly / Cal.com / TidyCal) — see `consultation.md`
   - The tracker from `templates/lead-tracker.csv` (Google Sheet or Notion)

**Exit:** promise sentence, 3 segments, handle secured, booking link live.

## §1. Benchmark (Days 3–10)

**Goal:** Copy the *system*, not the content, of people already getting this traffic.

Run `benchmarking.md`. Output: 10-row benchmark table, 3 outreach messages sent, 1 call with a creator or operator.

**Exit:** You can name the top 3 formats, the top 3 monetization paths, and the gap you'll fill.

---

## §2. Supply Side (Days 7–21, overlaps with §3)

**Goal:** Before you have leads, have somewhere to send them.

Target partner mix for the first 5:
- 1–2 licensed real-estate agents (공인중개사) who speak English or are foreigner-friendly
- 2 co-living / share-house operators or cohosts
- 1 adjacent business (moving company, SIM/phone shop, visa administrative agent 행정사, language school, co-working space)

Use `outreach-scripts.md` for the messages and the vetting checklist. At this stage, ask for **no money**: ask them to accept free intros and give your people a good experience. You'll charge later once you've proven lead quality.

**Exit:** 5 partners in `partner-directory.csv` marked `vetted = yes`.

---

## §3. Short-Form Engine (Days 10–40)

**Goal:** 30 shorts in 30 days. Volume first, then double down on what works.

Run `content-engine.md`. Weekly rhythm:
- **Mon:** write 7 scripts (Claude drafts, you add personal detail)
- **Tue:** batch film all 7 (1–2 hours)
- **Wed:** edit (CapCut / VN)
- **Thu–Sun + Mon–Wed:** post 1/day, reply to every comment within 24h, DM anyone who asks a question
- **Sun:** review numbers, pick 2 winners to remake

Every short ends with one CTA. Rotate: "comment ROOM and I'll send you my checklist," "link in bio for a free 15-min chat if you're already in Korea," "follow for part 2."

**Exit:** 30 posted; you know your top 3 hooks by retention/shares.

---

## §4. Coffee Chats (Days 20–60)

**Goal:** 15 chats. This is where trust is actually built.

Run `consultation.md`. In-person coffee if they're in your city; video call if not yet in Korea (still counts). Log every chat in the tracker.

**Exit:** 15 chats held, each with a logged "biggest problem" and a promised next step.

---

## §5. Free Value ×3 (Days 25–70)

**Goal:** Prove you really help, and document it.

For each promising lead, deliver and log 3 free wins. Examples:

| Win # | Example | Log as |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sent a personalized checklist (ARC, bank, phone, 보증금 explainer) for their visa + city | "Checklist sent, YYYY-MM-DD" |
| 2 | Sent a shortlist of 3 rooms/areas that match budget + commute | "Shortlist sent, they picked #2" |
| 3 | Warm intro to a vetted partner; viewing or call booked | "Intro to Host X, viewing Sat" |

Ask for a testimonial (text or 15-sec video) after win #3 — it becomes content.

Then state the paid step plainly:
> "I'm glad that helped. From here, if you want me to run the rest — viewings with you, translating the contract with a licensed agent, move-in day setup — that's my paid Move-In Package. Want the details?"

**Exit:** 5 people with 3 logged wins each.

---

## §6. Monetize (Days 40–90)

**Goal:** First paid customer on each side.

Run `pricing-and-offers.md` and pass the `compliance-korea.md` check. Two revenue lines:
- **Customer side:** fixed-price relocation service (not a % of rent)
- **Partner side:** flat per-qualified-lead fee, monthly featured-partner fee, or a formal referral arrangement with the licensed agent

Use a simple written agreement for every partner before the first paid intro.

**Exit:** ≥1 paid customer and ≥1 paying partner.

---

## §7. Validate: 10 Paying Customers (by ~Day 90–120)

**Goal:** Proof the model works beyond friends and luck.

Count a "paying customer" as either a paying relocator or a paying partner, but track them separately. Validation = **10 paid transactions in a rolling 90 days**, with at least 3 from people who found you through content (not personal network).

Weekly review questions (Claude should ask these):
1. How many new followers / profile visits / bookings / chats / wins / paid this week?
2. Which content piece drove the most bookings? Make 3 more like it.
3. Where did leads drop off? (views → bookings, chats → win #3, win #3 → paid)
4. Any partner complaints or no-shows? Remove bad partners fast.

**If you hit 10:** go to §8.
**If not by Day 120:** diagnose the weakest funnel step and run a 30-day fix sprint before changing the whole model.

---

## §8. Scale (after 10 paying customers)

**Goal:** Turn a working hustle into a legit business and maximize traffic → leads.

1. **Make it legal and clean:** business registration (사업자등록), bank account, invoicing, partner agreements, privacy policy. See `compliance-korea.md`.
2. **Long-form:** 1 YouTube video/week (area guides, cost breakdowns, "I helped X move in" case studies). Long-form converts better than shorts.
3. **Owned audience:** newsletter or KakaoTalk open chat community; a free downloadable "Moving to Korea" guide as the opt-in.
4. **SEO:** turn each long video into a blog post ("Goshiwon vs share house in Sinchon 2026").
5. **Productize:** tiered packages, a partner directory page with paid featured listings, group move-in webinars.
6. **Systemize:** templates for every message, a booking → intake form → CRM flow, a VA or part-timer for scheduling.
7. **Traffic goals:** set a monthly target for leads (bookings), not followers. Double down on the 1–2 channels producing bookings.
