# Benchmarking Existing Creators and Systems

Goal: find the people who already turn "moving to Korea" traffic into money, learn their system, and build a relationship with a few of them.

## 1. Where to Look

Search each platform with these terms (English + Korean):

- YouTube / TikTok / Instagram: `moving to Korea`, `living in Seoul`, `Seoul apartment tour`, `goshiwon`, `share house Seoul`, `ARC Korea`, `foreigner in Korea cost of living`, `Korea housing deposit`, `외국인 부동산`, `외국인 셰어하우스`
- Reddit: r/korea, r/Living_in_Korea, r/teachinginkorea, r/seoul — note who gets recommended repeatedly
- Facebook groups: expat housing groups for Seoul/Busan, teacher groups
- Naver blogs / cafes: foreigner-friendly agents (외국인 전문 공인중개사) often run blogs
- Co-living brands and share-house platforms that market to foreigners
- Newsletters / podcasts about expat life in Korea

Pick 10: ~6 creators, ~2 service businesses (co-living brands, relocation services), ~2 agents who market to foreigners.

## 2. What to Record (use `templates/benchmark-table.csv`)

For each one:

| Field | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Name / handle / link | |
| Platform + followers | Main platform and size |
| Avg views (last 10 posts) | Real reach, not follower count |
| Top 3 posts | Title/hook + views — these are proven topics |
| Formats | Talking head, POV, room tour, text-on-screen, vlog, street interview |
| Posting frequency | Per week |
| Funnel | What's in the bio link? Lead magnet? Booking? Newsletter? |
| Monetization | Ads, sponsorships, affiliate, own service, referral, community, course |
| Partners visible | Which housing / business brands do they feature? |
| Gap | What do comments ask that they don't answer? |
| Contact | Email / DM |

## 3. How Claude Should Analyze the Table

Produce:
1. **Top 3 formats** by average views
2. **Top 10 topics** (merge similar hooks)
3. **Monetization map** — who earns how, and which path fits a solo operator first
4. **Comment gold** — the 10 most repeated unanswered questions (these become your first scripts)
5. **Your gap** — the angle nobody owns that fits the user's personal taste (e.g., nobody does honest student-budget room tours in Sinchon; nobody explains 보증금 risks for E-2 teachers)

## 4. Getting Connected

Order of approach: operators/businesses first (they want leads), then mid-size creators (5k–100k, reachable), then large creators (only after you have something to offer).

What you offer them:
- **Operators / businesses:** free qualified leads while you test
- **Creators:** a collab video (you bring the Korean-local/housing expertise), a guest segment, answering their audience's housing questions, or a referral to your partners that pays them too
- **Everyone:** genuine engagement for 1–2 weeks before the ask (useful comments, shares)

Use the creator outreach scripts in `outreach-scripts.md`.

On the call, ask:
1. Where does most of your traffic that converts come from?
2. What do your followers ask you for that you don't offer?
3. How do you monetize, and what did you try that didn't work?
4. Which housing/business partners have you had good or bad experiences with?
5. Would you be open to a collab or referral arrangement?

## 5. Turning the Benchmark into Your Plan

- Copy **structures** (hook patterns, video length, posting cadence, funnel), never scripts or footage.
- Add your personal touch: your face, your neighborhood, your story, your opinion.
- Your first 10 scripts = the 10 "comment gold" questions answered better than anyone else.
