# Pricing and Offers

Run every offer through `compliance-korea.md` first. Prices below are starting points to test in KRW. Raise them after every 3 sales that close without pushback.

## When to Charge

```
Content (free) → Coffee chat (free) → Win #1 (free) → Win #2 (free) → Win #3 (free) → Paid offer
```

Tell people early that the first steps are free and end-to-end help is paid. It avoids awkwardness later.

## Customer-Side Offers (fixed price for your service, never a % of rent/deposit)

| Offer | What's included | Test price |
|---|---|---|
| **Move-In Starter** (remote, before arrival) | 45-min planning call, personalized area + budget plan, admin checklist, 3 vetted housing options with pros/cons | ₩99,000 |
| **Move-In Package** (in Korea) | Everything above + up to 2 viewing days with you as translator/companion, contract walkthrough alongside a licensed agent, move-in day setup (phone, bank, utilities registration help) | ₩290,000–₩490,000 |
| **Co-Living Placement** | Matching with a vetted co-living/share house, booking support, first-week check-in | ₩79,000–₩149,000 |
| **Hourly help** | Translation, calls with landlord, admin appointments | ₩40,000/hour |

Payment: upfront for Starter; 50/50 for the Package. Always issue a receipt. Once registered, issue proper invoices.

Guarantee (builds trust, low risk): "If I don't send you at least 3 options that fit your budget within 7 days, full refund."

## Partner-Side Offers

| Partner type | Model | Test price |
|---|---|---|
| Co-living / share house | Flat fee per qualified tenant who **moves in** (marketing/placement fee agreed in writing, with the operator as the lodging business) | ₩100,000–₩200,000 per move-in, or ~50% of first month for longer stays (decide with compliance check) |
| Co-living / business | **Featured partner** monthly fee: included in content, directory page, and recommended in chats (disclosed as ad) | ₩200,000–₩500,000/month once you have traffic |
| Licensed agent | Paid **marketing/advertising** arrangement (flat monthly or per qualified consultation lead), not a share of brokerage commission | ₩50,000–₩100,000 per qualified lead, or monthly retainer |
| Adjacent businesses | Affiliate code / per-customer fee / sponsorship of a video | Per partner |

Start partners on **free** intros (Phase 2). After 3 successful free intros to a partner, show them the results and propose a paid arrangement. Same "3 free wins" rule applies to the business side.

Script:
> "Over the last [X] weeks I sent you [N] people and [M] moved in. I'd like to keep sending you tenants and feature you in my content. From next month, my partner fee is [₩X per move-in / ₩X per month]. Does that work for you?"

## Charging Both Sides — Rules

1. **Disclose** to customers: "Some of the places I recommend pay me a partner fee. I only recommend places I've checked, and my price to you doesn't change."
2. **Customer fee = your service; partner fee = marketing / placement.** Don't describe either as a real-estate brokerage commission (중개보수).
3. Never recommend a worse option because it pays more. If a non-partner is the best fit, recommend it anyway. That's what keeps the business alive.

## Partner Agreement: Minimum Terms

- Parties, business registration numbers
- What you provide (content features, qualified introductions, what "qualified" means)
- Fee, trigger (e.g., tenant move-in date), payment terms (e.g., within 7 days, bank transfer, tax invoice)
- Refund/clawback if tenant leaves within X days
- Disclosure: partner acknowledges you disclose the relationship publicly
- Explicit statement that you don't act as a real-estate broker and all brokerage is handled by licensed agents
- Customer data handling and consent (see privacy)
- Termination: either side, 14 days' notice

Claude: when asked, draft the agreement in Korean + English and recommend review by a lawyer (변호사) or licensed 행정사 before first use.

## Validation Math (10 customers)

Example path to 10 paid transactions in 90 days:
- 90 shorts + 6 long-form → ~40 bookings → ~30 chats held → ~15 reach win #3 → ~6 buy customer-side offers
- 4 partners convert from free to paid after proven results
- = 10 paying customers; revenue ≈ ₩1.5–3M. Small, but proves the funnel. Scale comes from traffic, not price.
