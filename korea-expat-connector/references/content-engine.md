## Short-Form Script Format (25–45 sec)

```
TITLE (for you):
HOOK (0–2s, spoken + on-screen text):
PROBLEM (2–8s): the pain in their words
ANSWER (8–35s): 3 steps / 3 options / 1 surprising fact
PERSONAL TOUCH (1 line): a true detail from your life
CTA (last 3s): one only — comment keyword / link in bio for coffee chat / follow for part 2
ON-SCREEN TEXT: key lines
SHOT LIST: where to film, B-roll
CAPTION + 5 hashtags:
```

Claude: always fill every field. If you don't have a personal detail, ask the user for one.

## Filming Workflow

- Gear: phone, clip-on mic, window light. That's enough.
- Batch: film 7 in one session; change shirt/location every 2 to vary them.
- B-roll bank: film your neighborhood, doors, 부동산 office windows, subway, convenience store, room interiors (with permission). Reuse it.
- Captions on every video (most people watch muted).
- Post natively on each platform: TikTok, Reels, Shorts.

## Weekly Review → Double Down

Track per video: views, avg watch %, shares, saves, profile visits, comments with questions, bookings attributed. Each Sunday:
- Top 2 by shares/saves → make a part 2 or a remake with a new hook
- Bottom 2 → drop that topic or rewrite the hook
- Any question asked 3+ times → next week's script

## Long-Form (start ~week 6, 1/week)

Formats that convert viewers into bookings:
1. **Area guide (10–15 min):** "Living in Mapo as a foreigner: rent, vibe, commute, where I'd live."
2. **Cost breakdown:** real numbers, screenshots, spreadsheet.
3. **Case study:** "I helped [name] move from [country] to Seoul" — process, problems, result, their testimonial.
4. **Tour with partner:** co-living host or agent walkthrough (disclose the partnership).
5. **Complete guide:** "Renting in Korea as a foreigner — everything in one video" (evergreen SEO).

Long-form outline template:
```
Title (search-friendly) + thumbnail text (≤4 words)
0:00 Hook + promise
0:30 Who this is for
1:00 Section 1–4 (each: problem → explanation → example → mistake to avoid)
…    Personal story
…    Soft CTA mid-video: free checklist
End  Hard CTA: book a free 15-min coffee chat if you're in Korea / moving within 3 months
Description: timestamps, checklist link, booking link, partner disclosure
```

## Disclosure

Any content featuring a partner who pays you, or could pay you, must be marked as an ad/sponsorship. Korea's Fair Trade Commission (공정거래위원회) guidelines require clear disclosure of economic relationships ("광고", "유료 광고 포함"), and platforms require it too. Put it in the video and at the top of the caption.
