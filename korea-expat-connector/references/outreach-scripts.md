# Outreach Scripts + Partner Vetting

Always personalize line 1 with something specific and true. Keep first messages short. One ask per message.

## A. Creators / Influencers (benchmark phase)

**DM — first touch (after 1–2 weeks of genuine engagement):**
> Hey [Name], your video on [specific video] is the most honest take on [topic] I've seen, especially the part about [detail]. I'm a [Korean local / long-time Seoul resident] building free housing and move-in guides for foreigners. Would you be open to a 15-min call? I'd love to learn how you built your audience, and I can share what I'm seeing in housing questions from foreigners.

**Collab pitch (once you have ~20 posts):**
> Hey [Name], your comments are full of housing questions ([example comment]). I could answer the top 5 in a collab: you bring the foreigner's perspective, I bring the local rental know-how (deposits, contracts, scam red flags). I'll handle the research and script. Interested?

**Referral pitch (once partners are live):**
> When your followers ask where to live, I can take those questions off your plate. I do free 15-min chats and connect them with agents and co-living hosts I've personally checked. If any of them become paying clients, I'll share [X]% of my service fee with you. Want to try it with 3 people?

## B. Co-Living Hosts / Share-House Operators / Airbnb Cohosts

**First message (Korean + English, many hosts prefer Korean):**
> 안녕하세요, [이름] 님. [지역]에서 외국인 대상 이주 정보 콘텐츠를 만들고 있는 [본인 이름]입니다. 한국에 오는 외국인들이 방 구하는 문제로 많이 연락을 주시는데, 직접 확인한 좋은 숙소만 소개하고 싶습니다. 처음에는 비용 없이 입주 희망자를 소개해 드리고, 서로 잘 맞으면 이후에 정식 파트너십을 논의하고 싶습니다. 편하실 때 10분 통화 가능하실까요?
>
> Hi [Name], I make content helping foreigners move to [area]. I get many requests from people looking for rooms, and I only want to recommend places I've personally checked. I'd like to send you qualified tenants for free at first, and if it works well, talk about a formal partnership. Could we do a 10-min call or I visit the house?

**Visit questions:** occupancy and room prices, minimum stay, deposit and refund terms, contract language (English available?), house rules, who handles problems at 2am, foreigner-friendly payment, is the business properly registered/licensed for the stay type offered.

## C. Licensed Real-Estate Agents (공인중개사)

This partner matters most: it's how you stay legal on anything transaction-related.

> 안녕하세요, 대표님. 외국인 대상 한국 이주 콘텐츠를 운영하고 있는 [이름]입니다. 매달 [지역]에서 집을 구하는 외국인 문의가 들어오는데, 계약·중개는 반드시 공인중개사님을 통해 진행하려고 합니다. 외국인 고객 응대가 가능하신지, 제가 사전 상담과 통역·동행 서비스를 맡고 중개는 대표님께서 진행하시는 방식으로 협업이 가능하신지 여쭙고 싶습니다.
>
> Hello, I run content for foreigners moving to Korea and get monthly inquiries from people looking for homes in [area]. All contracts and brokerage should go through a licensed agent. I'd handle pre-consultation, interpretation and viewing support; you'd handle the brokerage. Can we talk about working together?

Ask: English support? typical commission (법정 중개보수)? do they check 등기부등본 and 전세 risk? can they add a clause for deposit protection? are they open to a paid marketing arrangement (flat monthly or per-lead, not a share of commission; see compliance)?

## D. Adjacent Businesses

Moving companies, phone/SIM shops, 행정사 (visa administrative agents), language schools, co-working spaces, furniture rental, cleaning.

> Hi [Name], I help foreigners settle in [city] through free content and coffee chats. Many need [their service] in the first month. I'd like to recommend a small number of businesses I trust. Could I try your service myself first? If it's great, I'll send people your way and we can discuss a partner arrangement later.

## E. Follow-Up Cadence

Day 0 message → Day 3 short bump ("Just floating this back up 🙂") → Day 10 value bump (share a relevant video or lead) → stop. Log every touch in `partner-directory.csv`.

## Partner Vetting Checklist (all must be "yes" before any intro)

- [ ] I visited in person or did a video walkthrough
- [ ] Business registration (사업자등록) and, where relevant, license checked (agent license number; stay-type registration for lodging)
- [ ] Clear written pricing, deposit and refund terms
- [ ] Contract available in English or they allow translation support
- [ ] Responds within 24h
- [ ] At least 3 positive reviews from foreigners, or I tried the service myself
- [ ] Agrees to treat my referrals fairly and tell me about problems
- [ ] Knows I disclose our relationship to customers

Remove a partner after one serious complaint (deposit issue, misrepresentation) or two minor ones.
